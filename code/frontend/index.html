AQMon Sydney

Air-quality monitoring & alert platform

Sydney air quality, processed in the cloud

Upload hourly readings from NSW Government monitoring stations (OpenAQ archive format). Files are queued, processed by serverless functions, stored in DynamoDB and turned into daily statistics, limit-exceedance alerts and charts.

Upload data

OpenAQ CSV files, max 5 MB each. Sample files are in data/sydney/ of the repository.

    Your account is view-only. Ask an administrator to add you to the uploaders group.
